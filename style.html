Style

Her style is typically colorful, bold and focused on telling a story. Her work often uses bright colors, clear typography and playful shapes. She also plays with overlapping and repeating of letter forms in her work.

Influences

In an interview she discusses that in 2005 she took a summer intensive at SVA with Milton Glaser and she created a 5-year plan for her life that fundamentally shifted the direction of her life and inspired her to teach others to create their own 10-year plan. She also mentions in the same interview that she is drawn to text-based art because “Text-based art utilizes both language and art to assess and interpret events, experiences, and concepts from minor moments of daily life to the grand nature of the human condition.”
