Career

20+ years at Sterling Brands: Led branding work for major companies and brands including Burger King, Hershey’s, Haagen-Dazs, Tropicana, and Star Wars. 

Design Matters: Founded and hosts the pioneering design podcast Design Matters, which won a 2011 Cooper Hewitt National Design Award and became one of the longest-running design podcasts. 

AIGA leadership: Served as President of AIGA, one of only five women to hold the position in its first 100 years, and received the AIGA Lifetime Achievement Award in 2019. 

Design education: Co-founded the world’s first graduate program in Branding at the School of Visual Arts (SVA).

Achievements

Artist & exhibitor: Her visual work has been exhibited at the Chicago Design Museum, Boston Biennale, SVA, and other major institutions. 

Harvard Business School: Became an Executive Fellow at Harvard Business School in 2024, following a case study on her career.
