About

Debbie is a writer, designer, educator, artist, brand consultant and host of the podcast ‘Design Matters’ which has been airing for 21 years now. The podcast is considered one of the first podcasts and it is the longest running podcast in the world. In 2010 she also co-founded the first graduate program in branding at the School of Visual Arts in New York City.

Background

Debbie was born in 1961 in Brooklyn, New York. She went to State University of New York at Albany where she got her Bachelor of Arts degree in English with a minor in Russian literature in 1983. While she was there she wrote for the student newspaper and became the arts and features editor her senior year. It would be this role that got her interested in design.
